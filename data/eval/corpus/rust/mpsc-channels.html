Message passing with mpsc channels

Channels

The std::sync::mpsc module provides a multiple-producer, single-consumer channel. The channel function returns a Sender and a Receiver. The sender can be cloned and given to several threads, while the receiver stays with one consumer.

Sending and receiving

The send method moves a value into the channel and recv blocks until a value arrives or every sender is dropped. Iterating over the receiver is a convenient way to process messages until the channel closes.

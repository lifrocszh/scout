Vec::drain

Overview

The drain method removes a contiguous range of elements from a vector and returns an iterator over the removed items. The vector is shortened immediately and the tail is shifted down when the iterator is dropped.

Example

let mut letters = vec!['a', 'b', 'c', 'd', 'e'];
let middle: Vec<char> = letters.drain(1..4).collect();
assert_eq!(letters, ['a', 'e']);

Notes

Draining the full range with drain(..) empties the vector while keeping its allocated capacity, which makes it a cheap way to move every item into another collection.

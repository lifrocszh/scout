PersistentVolumes and claims

Overview

A PersistentVolume is a piece of cluster storage, and a PersistentVolumeClaim is a request for storage by a user. Binding a claim to a volume decouples applications from the underlying disk.

Provisioning

A StorageClass lets the cluster create volumes dynamically when a claim is made. The reclaim policy decides whether the data is kept or deleted after the claim is released.

asyncio.gather

Running coroutines concurrently

The gather function schedules several awaitables at once and returns their results as a list in the same order as the arguments. Total time is close to that of the slowest coroutine instead of the sum of all of them.

Errors

By default the first exception is raised to the caller while the other tasks keep running. With return_exceptions=True, exceptions are returned in the results list so one failure does not hide the rest.

Full text search

Overview

Full text search matches documents against a query by words rather than by exact text. A document is turned into a tsvector of normalised lexemes with to_tsvector, and a query becomes a tsquery with to_tsquery or websearch_to_tsquery.

Indexing and ranking

A GIN index on a tsvector column speeds up the @@ match operator, and ts_rank orders results by relevance.

Tutorial: scaling consumers

Consumer groups

Consumers that share a group name split the partitions of a topic between them. Start a second consumer in the same group and the broker triggers a rebalance.

Rebalance behaviour

During a rebalance each consumer briefly pauses while partitions are reassigned. Keep groups stable and add consumers one at a time to avoid repeated rebalances.
